I'm a student in the **IIT Madras BS Aeronautics/Space Technology** program who learns by building — not just reading. Over the past several years that's meant 3D-printing and assembling a working telescope, designing a conceptual satellite system around a Raspberry Pi, running an aquaponics ecosystem, and — most recently — building **local AI agents** that read email, summarize it, and talk back through Discord, running entirely on a Raspberry Pi.

I write up most of what I build on Medium, partly to document it properly and partly because explaining a thing is the fastest way to find out if you actually understood it.
