I'm an **Aeronautics & Space Technology** student at **IIT Madras** (BS), building from Chennai. For about five years, well before any of it was coursework, I've worked where space science, AI and hardware overlap: a student satellite that flew on India's first private rocket, a telescope I printed and wired myself, an aquaponics loop, and now AI agents that live on a Raspberry Pi.

My loop is simple: take a hard problem apart, build something real with what I learn, then write about it.

## Hardware and space

- **Satellite on Vikram-S.** Designed through Space Kidz India; it flew on India's first private rocket.
- **Femto-satellite payloads, Rameswaram 2021.** Helped design and assemble ultra-light payloads for a multi-payload student launch.
- **Raspberry Pi telescope.** Mostly 3D-printed, Pi-driven, and usable on its own up to 160x zoom. [Write-up](https://medium.com/@aaravgandhi/stargazing-on-a-budget-my-middle-school-project-6f44ee8a0232)
- **Conceptual satellite system.** Structure, subsystems, power and telemetry, built around a Pi with multiple sensors and cameras.
- **Amateur rocketry.** Custom-designed PCBs and 3D-printed parts.
- **3D printing, commercially.** 300+ designs sold, with print monitoring from anywhere through OctoPrint.
- **Audio-reactive T-shirt.** Arduino/ESP32, NeoPixels and a microphone.
- **Aquaponics.** Fish and plants sustaining each other in a closed loop. [Write-up](https://medium.com/@aaravgandhi/my-budget-lockdown-project-aquaponics-a9722ed1bbb8)
